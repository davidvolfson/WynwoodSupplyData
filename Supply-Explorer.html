Student housing assumptions

Shared/undergrad and graduate solo: up to $2,500 rent per person per month, one person per legal bedroom. For shared apartments, divide whole-unit rent by the number of legal bedrooms; private-room quotes are already per person. Utilities and fees are additional. Promotional/effective quotes are explicitly identified and normal paid-month rent must be confirmed. Allow another $200/person shared or $250 solo for utilities, internet, insurance and fees; parking and move-in costs are extra. Assume roughly 12 months and an accepted guarantor or qualifying income.
